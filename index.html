CBC Clip Uploader

CBC Clip Uploader is a small personal tool used by the owner of the Certified By Chat channel. It uploads short gaming clips made for the channel to the channel's own TikTok account, as a draft or a post, only after the owner approves each video.

It uses TikTok Login Kit to connect the owner's own account and the TikTok Content Posting API to upload videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
