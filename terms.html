About the tool

CBC Clip Uploader ("the tool") is a private, non-commercial utility run by the owner of the Certified By Chat channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the tool does

  • Signs in to the channel owner's own TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit.
  • Uploads a finished short video to that account through the TikTok Content Posting API, as a draft in the account's inbox or as a post.
  • Never uploads anything without the owner's explicit approval of that specific video.

Use

The tool is used only with the owner's own account. Videos uploaded with it follow TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service, credit the original creators, and are labeled as AI-assisted where TikTok requires it.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is. Its access can be removed at any time in TikTok's settings (Manage app permissions).
